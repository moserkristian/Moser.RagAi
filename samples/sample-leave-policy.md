# Sample leave policy

Full-time staff accrue 20 days of paid leave in each calendar year.
A leave request has to be sent at least 10 working days before the first day off.
Up to 5 unused days carry into the next calendar year. Further unused days are dropped on 31 March.
Sick leave is separate and is not counted against the 20 days.
